# Research History: $ARGUMENTS

Research historical Middle East events from the 1970s-1990s era for game content inspiration. Argument should be the topic (e.g., "1982 Lebanon War" or "Israeli nuclear program").

## Research Goals

Find information relevant to creating authentic game content:
- Real events that could inspire game events
- Historical accuracy for weapon/country data
- Political dynamics between nations
- Consequences of various actions

## Steps

### 1. Web Search

Search for authoritative sources:
- Wikipedia articles
- Academic sources
- News archives (NYT, BBC, etc.)
- Military history sites

### 2. Extract Relevant Details

For **military events**:
- Date and duration
- Countries/factions involved
- Weapons and tactics used
- Casualties and outcomes
- Political consequences

For **political events**:
- Key figures involved
- Trigger conditions
- International response
- Long-term effects
- Related events

For **diplomatic events**:
- Parties involved
- Issues at stake
- Outcome/agreement
- Implementation success

### 3. Game Relevance Analysis

Analyze how this could translate to game mechanics:

```markdown

- Category: {crisis/opportunity/diplomatic/military/internal/international}

- Consequences: {relationship/stability/budget effects}

- {What to simplify for gameplay}

- {Events that could lead to this}
```

## Sensitivity Guidelines

Remember this game deals with real conflicts. When researching:

- Focus on strategic/political aspects, not graphic violence
- Note civilian impact factually, not sensationally  
- Present multiple perspectives where relevant
- Flag topics that need careful handling in-game
- Avoid reinforcing stereotypes
